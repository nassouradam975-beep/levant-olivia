Our story

One grove, three generations.

“If you want to get rich quick, don't plant olive trees.” – Grandfather Amin

My grandfather Amin has spent his whole life among olive trees. His father harvested olives in Latakia before him, and every autumn the whole family comes together to pick.

For us, this oil was never a product. It was breakfast with za'atar and bread, it was my grandmother's cooking, it was home. When we came to Hamburg, we always missed it, because you can't find oil like this in a supermarket here.

With Levant Olivia we're sharing it with you: the same oil we keep at home, bottled for your table.
